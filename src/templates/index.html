Welcome to Coastal Edge

A new home at the shore, built for every season

Coastal Edge is a new five story building at 401 Boulevard, between the ocean and Barnegat Bay. Thirty six residences sit above the street, each with an open kitchen, wide sliding doors and a private deck.

Come down for the summer or stay all year. The pool is indoors and heated, parking is covered, and the beach and boardwalk are a short walk from the lobby.
